What changed in round 4

  • Stamp · subtle: the stamp edge kept, but the bites drop from 5px every 15px to 3px every 10px, and the stub tint is lighter. It still reads as a stamp, closer to a fine perforation than a postage-stamp scallop. Now the default.
  • Symbol toggle: Centre (default) or Foot.
  • Horizontal motion only: Feed and Line print are gone, since the ticket is sideways. Print across replaces them: a print head runs left to right in small steps, the same direction the ticket reads and tears.
  • Punched is removed; it wasn't in your shortlist.

Ticket styles

Stamp · subtle · 9.5The charm of SW-02/04 at SW-05/06 volume. The edge only shows itself up close, which suits a section between two busy ones. My pick.
Stamp edge · 9The full scallop. Most ownable, a touch loud next to R2-E.
Pinhole · 9Quietest. Only the tear lines are perforated.
Notched · 8Instantly a ticket, but the most expected.

Symbol

Centre · 9Sits on the ticket's middle line, so it balances the three columns and lines up with the notches. Reads as the ticket's seal.
Foot · 8.5Reads as a printed issuer mark. Quieter, but leaves the stub looking empty above it.

Animations (all left to right)

Write-in · 9.5Your pick. Each field writes in left to right, then the stamp lands.
Print across · 8.5A print head crosses the ticket in 30 steps. More mechanical; good if you want an "issued" feeling.
Unroll + write · 8The ticket unrolls from the stub, then writes in.

Figma build notes

  • Ticket: x 580, y 144. Body 720 × 500 (stub 56 + three fields), tear-off 152 × 500, Station Yellow.
  • Stamp · subtle: 3px radius bites every 10px on all outer edges and both sides of the seam. Stamp edge: 5px every 15px. Stub fill #EAE1CF.
  • Symbol: 24px, 60% Carbon, centred in the stub (or 34px from the bottom for Foot).
  • Tear on hover: translate 6, 2 and rotate 2° around the bottom-left corner, 500ms.
  • Write-in: each title and line reveals left to right over 700ms, fields 600ms apart, stamp at 1.9s. Once, on entry; reduced motion shows the end state.
