Privacy.

We ask for as little as we can, use it only to do the job you asked for, and never sell it.

Who we are

ONAWAY is a locally owned cleaning, moving and delivery company serving Toronto and the GTA. [Registered business name, address.] Questions about your information go to privacy@onaway.ca [or hello@onaway.ca].

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote: your name, phone number, email, whether it’s for a home or a business (and the company name if it’s a business), the service, the area or postal code, the size of the job, timing, how you’d like us to reach you, and anything you add in the notes.
  • When you book with us: the address and details we need to do the job and invoice you.
  • Automatically: your IP address and the time of your request, used only to stop spam and abuse.

We don’t ask for your full address until you book, and we never ask for payment details through the website.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your quote and send it to you in writing.
  • To contact you about your request, the way you asked us to.
  • To schedule, carry out and invoice the job if you book.
  • To keep the site free of spam.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your information. We don’t send marketing emails or texts. If that ever changes, we’ll only send them to people who’ve said yes.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted services to run the business. They only see what they need, and only to provide their service:

  • [Hosting provider], which hosts this website and our email.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that quote requests come from people, not bots.
  • Telegram, which alerts our team when a new request arrives.
  • Trello, where our team tracks requests.

Some of these services may store information outside Canada, where it’s protected by their own security commitments and may be subject to local laws.

How long we keep it

  • Requests that don’t become a booking: deleted after [12] months.
  • Booked jobs and invoices: kept for [7] years, because Canadian tax law requires us to keep business records.

Keeping it safe

Your information is sent over an encrypted connection, kept on password-protected systems, and seen only by the people who need it to quote and do your job.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • See what we hold: you can ask what information we have about you.
  • Correct it: you can ask us to fix anything that’s wrong.
  • Delete it: you can ask us to delete it, unless we’re required to keep it.

Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date below.
