When it runs

  • Every navigation that isn't to a service page (PT-01) or the quote page (PT-02): Home, About, Help, Privacy, Accessibility, the 404, and back and forward.
  • The blocks are each page's main pieces (heading, lead, image, first section), marked once in the templates.
  • Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade.

The system

  • Three types, picked from the link's address before the page leaves: service, quote, general.
  • One mechanism: native cross-document View Transitions plus about 1 KB of JS. No framework; Lenis and GSAP keep working.
  • The navbar is named once and never animates; no transition relies on it being in view.
  • One easing family: an even curve for things that travel, a long soft ease-out for things settling, nothing that bounces.
