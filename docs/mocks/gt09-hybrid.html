WHERE WE’LL BE

Idea

The hybrid. GT-06's sentence, "We'll be in ___ on your date.", sits on GT-01's map, now near full-bleed on a plate that mirrors the hero's. The two are wired together: while idle, the sentence tours ten places and the map flies to each one, so the words and the city move as one. Type your postal code and the map flies to your place, drops a yellow pin, tints your region, and the sentence writes your place in with a highlighter under it. The page opens on a plate of the city from the air and closes on a plate of the city as a map, with you on it.

  • Map layers: region tint (yellow 22% on match) · minor streets 0.6px 13% · rivers 5px Camel 50% · concession roads 1px 42% · regional boundaries 2/6 dotted · highways 5px Carbon 75% with 1.6px centre line · shoreline 1.6px · labels (roads mono 9, highways mono 10, regions mono 16 at 28%, places Body 13).
  • Idle: Tour flies to each of ten places at 1.5× every 3.4s; Still keeps the map wide and only hops the pin. Reduced motion: no flying; cut straight to the end state.
