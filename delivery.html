Questions.What people ask most. More on the Help page.

How big can an item be?
If two people can carry it through a standard door, we can deliver it. Anything bigger or heavier, tell us in your quote and we’ll say straight.
Can you pick up from a store or a Marketplace seller?
Yes. Give us the address and a contact, and we’ll check the item when we collect it.
What if no one’s home?
Tell us where it’s safe to leave, or who can sign. If there’s nowhere safe, we call you and rebook the window.
Do you take things upstairs?
Yes, into the room you choose. Tell us about stairs or an elevator in your quote.
Is it insured on the way?
Yes, from pickup until it’s placed. Your written quote says how.
