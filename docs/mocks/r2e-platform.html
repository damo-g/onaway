The idea

Section 02. It opens on three station signs hanging from one beam on EL-01 (light environment) or ED-03 (dark environment). On scroll the backdrop fades out; the header and section keep the environment colour, and the signs keep their own colour as they widen into the board rows.

Every element keeps its side for the whole scroll, whichever icon side is chosen. Only the description and the fact column are new, and they appear in the middle near the end. Use the toggles above to switch environment, sign colour, arrangement and icon side, then press Play or drag the slider.

What changed from round 1

  • One mode per section: dark stays dark and light stays light; only the backdrop fades.
  • Position-locked morph: tile, name and arrow never change sides.
  • Pictograms locked: spray bottle, tilted dolly, front-view box.
  • Copy updated: Homes and workplaces, once or on a schedule · Homes and offices, one room to a full floor · Single items to full loads, same-day or scheduled. Delivery fact is now GTA-WIDE.
  • Hover works from the start (face inverts, sign lifts 3px) and on the end board; it pauses during the morph.
  • Arrows toggle: all → or angled ↗ → ↘. Fact toggle: fixed fact or next open window. Description toggle: its own column, or tucked under the service name.
  • Environment (header, background, plate) and sign colour are separate toggles, giving four combinations.
  • Icon side: left (icon scanned first) or right (word first, arrow, then the icon tile), carried through to the end state.
  • Two arrangements, both hanging from the beam: chained (100/93/87%, each sign hangs from the one above) or perspective (100/90/82%, 21px gaps, every sign on its own rods down from the beam, back signs dimmed, shadows lighter and softer toward the back).

Figma build notes

  • Plates: EL-01 and ED-03 at 1512 wide, plate top at y 233, beam at 300–456 in both arrangements.
  • Chained signs: 900 × 104, 840 × 96, 780 × 90, centred. Perspective: 940 × 112, 846 × 100, 770 × 90, 21px gaps, rods 5 / 4 / 3px, depth dimmed to 90% and 82%.
  • Icon right: name at x 64 in the end state, description at x 420, fact at x 900, arrow, then the 144 tile inset 64 from the right.
  • Sign: Carbon housing 6px, 8px radius. Dark: Carbon face with a 22% Ticket Paper edge. Light: Ticket Paper face. Tile on the left, square, inset 12; name at 38% of sign height; arrow inset 34 on the right.
  • End rows match R2-D: 1512 × 166 from y 330; tile 144 at x 64; name 55; description at x 552; fact at x 970; arrow inset 64.
  • Scroll over about 800px, pinned: 0–0.55 backdrop fades; 0.12–0.8 signs move; 0.62–1 details fade in. Hover is live at rest (start and end) and paused mid-morph.
