Figma build notes

  • LOCKED: Carbon · Hanging · Windows fill · Plain · "We'll take it from here." · Arrive · Signal hover.
  • Shaders, all drawn live in WebGL with the photos' film grain, in the brand palette: Sky (the hero's overcast drifting across the letters), Lake (Lake Ontario's slow swell with paper glints), Traffic (the Gardiner at dusk: yellow and rust lights streaming in opposite lanes), Flap (a departure board of tiles turning over in Carbon, Camel, Paper and Rust) and Windows (the city's towers with lights coming on one by one). Reduced motion shows a still frame. The earlier abstract gradient is gone.
  • Hover: Sweep (Carbon fills the row left to right and the text turns Paper; on Carbon it's the reverse) · Invert (the same colours, instantly) · Mark (a 50% yellow highlighter under the text, as in QC-02) · Signal (the yellow square drops in at the start of the row and the text steps 21px right).
  • Rows keep 1px hairlines; the hover fill extends 10px past each side of the row.
  • Signal: a Station Yellow square that turns 45° every 2.1s (hold, turn, hold), like Menkind's turning dot; Still keeps it square; Off removes it. Reduced motion: still.
  • Wordmark: the T2 wordmark as a mask filled with the foreground colour, so it recolours with each palette.
