What changed in round 3

  • One curve for anything that travels (an even ease, so the eye can follow it), a long soft ease-out for things settling.
  • The rail is never drawn in empty space any more: in A2 it grows out from the sign's spot, in D2 it comes out of the card itself.
  • The hero's mini cards and the drop rows do exactly the same (From toggle); links without a card get a short crossfade with the rail and doors.

How it would be built

  • The card and the band share a view-transition-name; D2's rail is a second named piece (the card's top edge ↔ the page's rail), so the browser carries both at once.
  • The doors' hang is the page's own entrance animation, softened.
  • Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade. Firefox: a normal page load.
