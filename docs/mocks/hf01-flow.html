What changed

  • Both: the navbar sits flush on the fold in A, the nav links are centred on the page in every state, and the logo always settles at 80%.
  • Pinned: zoom defaults to 1.00 → 1.25. The descriptor is gone before the navbar reaches it. The GTA tag disappears the moment the navbar crosses it, and the service cards now arrive straight after docking, alongside the headline, instead of after it.
  • Scroll-past: the headline block hangs a fixed 78px under the navbar the whole way up, so it never peeks out in A and the gap to ONAWAY never changes. The coordinates and the "Next" line are gone. Places change on their own every 2.6 seconds once you arrive.

Place effects, ranked

Split-flap · 9Completes the sentence on a departure board: "at your door in" then the place flips in, tile by tile. The most on-brand of the four and the easiest to scan. Risk: if it flips too often it becomes a toy; keep it at 2.5–3 s.
Ticker band · 8.5Your earlier aesthetic: a curved band of place names drifting past. Ambient and calm, and it never asks to be read. It doesn't complete the sentence, so the headline drops "in".
Roll · 8The previous list, now automatic. Clear, but the stack of faded names below is the busiest of the four.
Drum · 7.5A 3D cylinder. Striking, but the tilted words are hard to read and it leans toward a tech demo.
