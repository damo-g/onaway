Idea

Locked direction, round 4 polish. Backgrounds are now six faint materials and marks from the job itself: moving-blanket Quilt, a Ticket sheet of blank stubs, Perforation (just the sheet's tear lines), a Route that draws one journey across the section and then keeps flowing (its dotted trail drifts along the line and a small square travels it once every 46 seconds), Cardboard flutes with two strips of packing tape, and a slow field of wayfinding Arrows. The form guides you too: the next empty blank breathes its underline, the button says what it needs ("Pick a service to start", "Add your area", then "Get my free quote"), and while it's waiting it stays yellow at 70% rather than turning grey, so the state change is a brightening, not a colour swap.

How much to ask

1 · ServiceThe smallest possible ask: one tap. The most people start, but the quote page then has to ask everything else, and some will stop there.
2 · + AreaService plus where. Enough to confirm coverage and route the request to the right crew, so the quote page only needs contact details and specifics. Usually pre-filled from the map, so it's still one tap for most people.
3 · + WhenAdds a rough time as one tap (This week / Next week / Later / Not sure yet). No calendar, so it's low effort, and it lets us prioritise. My pick: two required answers, one optional tap.

Either way the homepage never asks for a name, phone or photos; those live on the quote page. Hours are assumed as 8 AM to 8 PM, Toronto time; after 6 PM the promise rolls to 10 AM the next morning.
