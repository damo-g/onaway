# ONAWAY Brand Guide

The working rules for the ONAWAY brand: name, voice, colour, type, logo and imagery.

## The name

**ONAWAY.** "Ona" comes from the founder's surname, Onabule. In Yoruba, *ọ̀nà* means road or way. In English it reads as "on our way."

- Logo and display: **ONAWAY**, all caps.
- In a sentence: **Onaway**. Never OnAway, On-a-way or OnaWay.

## Positioning and personality

Onaway is the reliable, approachable local option for cleaning, moving and delivery across the GTA. It isn't the cheapest or the biggest.

- **Archetype:** Everyman + Caregiver, run with the precision of a transit system. That precision shows in the layout, never in the tone.
- **Promise:** order and precision. We bring a plan to every job and do what we said, when we said.
- **Brand words:** organized, grounded, approachable, precise, dependable, local, clear, warm, purposeful, dynamic.
- **Feels like:** a helpful neighbour who runs a tight schedule.

## Tagline and voice

**Tagline: The GTA's steady hands.** It goes in sign-offs, the footer, on vans and uniforms. It is never used next to a headline that already says "steady hands."

**Voice:** direct, warm, confident, local. Short sentences and plain words. Use specifics (times, places, what's included) instead of adjectives.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "We arrive inside the window." | "Seamless, stress-free service!" |
| Real GTA references: condos, elevator bookings, the Gardiner | Generic "your city" copy |
| Promise only what the crew actually does | Hedged or vague promises |

## Colour

| Colour | Hex | Role | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ticket Paper | #F7F2E7 | Base and backgrounds | Use this instead of pure white |
| Carbon | #202521 | Text, logo, dark sections | 13.95:1 on Ticket Paper |
| Station Yellow | #E4BB3F | Signal: the quote button, highlights, route markers | Never text on Ticket Paper (1.64:1). Carbon text on yellow is fine (8.52:1). Never in the logo |
| Rust Red | #A9503E | Warm accent: links, emphasis, "GTA" in the tagline, the evening version | Passes AA on Ticket Paper (4.82:1) |
| Camel | #B49A78 | Neutral: dividers, textures, illustration, the logomark gradient | Decorative only. Never text (2.40:1) |

Mostly Ticket Paper, then Carbon, with Station Yellow and Rust Red used sparingly. No more than three Station Yellow moments per screen.

## Typography

Display, headings and body use Instrument Sans SemiCondensed. Labels, data and the tagline use Overpass Mono. The logo is custom lettering, not set type. Sizes: Display 110 caps · H1 68 caps · H2 42 · H3 26 · Lead 20 · Body 16 · Small 14 · Label 12 mono. Arrows are Instrument Sans glyphs (← ↑ → ↓ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙).

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** custom ONAWAY lettering with an arched W, straight stems and soft arches, all caps. Two optical cuts: Text below about 56px and Display above.
- **Tagline lockup:** "THE GTA'S STEADY HANDS" in Overpass Mono caps under the wordmark, exactly as wide as the wordmark, with only "GTA" in Rust Red.
- **Symbol:** the arched W inside a true circle. The W's tops are cut at a slight angle, left lower than right. The gradient lives only on the W.

| Version | Circle | W | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Flat Carbon | Grain gradient, Ticket Paper to Camel | Default on light |
| Dark | Flat Ticket Paper | Grain gradient, Carbon to Rust Red | Dark backgrounds |
| Hero | Gradient, Carbon to Rust Red | Grain gradient, Camel to Ticket Paper | Large, special moments |
| Flat | Carbon, or Paper with a thin Carbon outline | Solid | Below 32px |

Station Yellow is never used in the logo.

## Grid and spacing

- **Columns:** 12 on desktop, 8 on tablet, 4 on mobile.
- **Margins:** 20 on desktop, 40 on tablet, 20 on mobile.
- **Gutters:** 24, 20 and 16.
- **Splits:** 7/5 and 8/4.
- **Spacing scale:** 8 · 13 · 21 · 34 · 55 · 89 · 144. Leave generous negative space.

## Graphic language and imagery

The visual idea is a transit system for everyday life: routes, stops and arrival boards.

- **Motifs:** route lines with numbered stops, directional arrows, wayfinding signs, modular cards, container shapes with notch cutouts.
- **Wayfinding UI:** word first, arrow second, icon in its own tile. Thin rules divide a sign into zones.
- **Ticket perforation:** two web moments only, the quote success state and the move-day pass.
- **Illustration over photography.** Grain-gradient painting for about 80% of imagery. Dot matrix for about 20%, only for numbers, times, the route line and departure-board moments. Both styles use only the five palette colours.

## Avoid

- Navy and orange together.
- Pure black on pure white.
- Flat or cartoonish illustration, clip-art, mascots, literal truck icons.
- Dither as decoration.
- Parallax, retro Toronto clichés, soft pastels.
- Station Yellow in the logo or as text on paper.
- Vague or clichéd lines.
- Small all-caps eyebrow labels everywhere.
