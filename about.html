Hope is not a plan.

Most people only call for help a handful of times: a move, a deep clean before the keys go back, a sofa from across town. Each time, they let strangers into their home or their business and hope it goes well.

We started Onaway to replace that hope with a plan. A written quote with every cost on it. An arrival window we keep. A crew that treats your place like a workplace with rules, whether we’re carrying, cleaning or delivering.

We’re from here. We know which buildings need an elevator booked, which condos want a certificate of insurance, and how long the Gardiner takes at four.

Our fine print
is the big print.

Most small print protects the company. Ours is six promises to you, in the largest type on the page.

Every job begins with a request and ends with your signature, and between the two we commit to the following on every move, every clean and every delivery, for homes and for businesses alike, wherever in the Greater Toronto Area the work takes us. 01Nothing starts until it’s in writing. Prices are set out in the written quote and are not changed on the day without your agreement. 02We arrive inside the window. Arrival is within the stated window; where traffic, weather or a building intervenes, notice is given in advance and not after the fact. 03Protect first, then work. Before anything is lifted, scrubbed or set down, floors, frames and finishes are protected with runners, pads and covers suited to the space. 04One person answers. A single named contact is assigned to the job and is reachable from the quote to completion. 05We leave when you’ve checked. At completion the work is inspected with you and any omission is corrected before the crew departs. 06If we can’t, we say so. Where a request is outside what we can do well, we say so plainly and, where we can, refer you to someone who can.

The Onaway standard · Applies to moving, cleaning and delivery · Homes and businesses
