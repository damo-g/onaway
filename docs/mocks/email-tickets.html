How it holds up in inboxes

  • The perforation is small background tiles. Gmail, Apple Mail, iOS and Outlook.com show it. Outlook desktop shows a straight edge, and the notches become square corners.
  • The clock is a PNG (Gmail shows no SVG) drawn from the Hugeicons clock-01 path.
  • The dot matrix is table cells, not an image, so it shows with images off. The board email is about 50 KB, well under Gmail’s 102 KB clip.
  • The plain-text version still goes with every email.
