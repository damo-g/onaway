Accessibility.

Everyone should be able to get a quote and get the job done. If something gets in your way, tell us and we’ll fix it.

Our website

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. That means:

  • Every page and the quote form work with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline.
  • Pages are built so screen readers can follow them, and images have text descriptions.
  • Text and buttons have strong colour contrast.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off.
  • Pages still work when you zoom in to 200%.

Get a quote another way

If the form doesn’t work for you, you don’t need it. Call or text +1 647 877 1411, or email hello@onaway.ca, any day from 8 AM to 8 PM, and we’ll take your request directly.

On the job

Let us know about anything that helps us serve you well: a support person or service animal coming along, a preferred way to communicate, or access needs at your home or business. We’ll plan around it.

Tell us what isn’t working

If you find a barrier on this site or with our service, email hello@onaway.ca, call or text, or tell any member of our team. Let us know the page or situation and what happened. We’ll reply within 2 business days and tell you what we’re doing about it. We can send information in another format if you need one.

Changes

We check this site as we add to it and update this page when things change.
